# Ekhaya Creator & Strategic Partner Programme

## Purpose

Grow Ekhaya Marketplace Price Checker through trusted relationships with people and businesses already serving South African marketplace sellers. The programme is designed around complementary value, not treating every seller-service provider as a competitor.

## Partner categories

1. **Creators and influencers** — TikTok creators, YouTubers and social personalities covering marketplace selling, sourcing, product research and entrepreneurship.
2. **Marketplace educators and communities** — coaches, training providers, seller groups and communities.
3. **Sourcing and supplier partners** — wholesalers, distributors, importers and sourcing businesses able to provide relevant product catalogues or supplier pricing.
4. **Technology and ecosystem partners** — seller software providers and services that complement product research and buying decisions.

## Initial collaboration formats

- Honest product demonstrations and tutorial content.
- Co-created educational sessions and seller workshops.
- Referral campaigns using unique partner attribution.
- Supplier catalogue or pricing collaborations.
- Complementary integrations with seller tools and services.

## Commercial principles

- Start with a small number of relevant founding partners and learn from pilot campaigns.
- Agree each pilot's deliverables, attribution window, reward/commission, payment timing, disclosure requirements and termination terms in writing before launch.
- Do not publish a universal commission rate until unit economics, subscription payment handling, refunds and chargebacks have been modelled.
- Do not promise creators automatic tracking or commission payments until the relevant backend is implemented and tested.
- Use transparent referral disclosures and require creators to give honest opinions; no guaranteed results or earnings claims.
- Keep partner access limited: no partner should see customer personal information or another partner's performance data.

## Suggested first pilot

Approach a small group of marketplace-focused creators and educators, including Beyond Shopping as a potential conversation. Offer a guided demonstration account when the product is ready, a short product walkthrough, and a jointly agreed pilot. Measure qualified registrations, activated users, paid conversions, retention and support effort—not views alone.

## Product and engineering roadmap

### Stage 1 — conversation and manual pilot
- Public partnership overview page at /partners.
- Founder-led outreach and individually agreed pilot terms.
- Manual attribution recorded in a controlled partner register.
- No automated commission promise.

### Stage 2 — attribution foundation
- User accounts and persistent database.
- Partner records with unique referral codes/links.
- Referral attribution stored at registration and retained through checkout.
- Attribution rules for first-touch/last-touch, expiry and self-referrals.
- Consent, privacy notice and partner terms.
- Admin view and auditable referral events.

### Stage 3 — commission and reporting
- Commission rules by campaign/partner.
- Calculate only on eligible settled subscription payments.
- Define treatment of discounts, refunds, chargebacks, cancellations and failed renewals.
- Partner dashboard with clicks, sign-ups, conversions, eligible commission and payout status.
- Admin approval and payout ledger; downloadable statements.
- Fraud checks and role-based access.

### Stage 4 — scale
- Campaign assets and demo environments.
- Partner tiers only if they add clear value.
- Cohort reporting for conversion and retention.
- Potential integrations with creator/affiliate platforms where commercially justified.

## Important current limitations

The existing Price Checker MVP does not yet have production authentication, a persistent customer database, subscription checkout, verified payment webhooks, referral attribution or commission accounting. The /partners page is an informational preview only. It does not accept applications, create referral links, track clicks, or calculate/pay commissions.

Do not store partner codes or commission balances as trusted values in browser localStorage. Referral and commission records must be enforced server-side and persisted in a database before the programme is opened for self-service registration.

## Success measures

- Relevant partner conversations started.
- Qualified seller registrations attributed to each partner.
- First successful product comparisons.
- Trial-to-paid conversion.
- Subscriber retention and net revenue after partner rewards.
- Support burden and quality of referred users.
